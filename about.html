THE AUTHOR

Meet Bethany

I’m Bethany Simon, creator of Bramblewood Books and author of Finn the Fox Who Was Afraid of the Dark.

Bramblewood began with a simple idea: to help children explore big feelings through stories of kindness, curiosity and friendship.

I live in West Sussex, where countryside walks and the changing seasons inspire my writing. When I’m not outdoors, I’m often dreaming up the next adventure for Finn and his friends.

I hope these stories become familiar favourites in your home.

With love,
Bethany
