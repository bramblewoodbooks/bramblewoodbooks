ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Bethany Simon

Bethany created Bramblewood as a warm woodland world where young readers can explore courage, friendship, kindness and growing confidence through gentle adventures.

Her first book, Finn the Fox Who Was Afraid of the Dark, is Book One of the Bramblewood Series.
