BOOK ONE

Finn the Fox Who Was Afraid of the Dark

A gentle story about seeing the dark differently.

Finn loves Bramblewood — its streams, its paths and its little wonders — but when evening comes, everything feels different. With Pip, Harry and Olive beside him, Finn begins to discover that the night does not make the woodland disappear; it simply helps different things appear.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Bethany Simon

Bethany Simon is the creator of Bramblewood Books, a woodland story world filled with friendship, curiosity and gentle lessons for young readers.

Her first book, Finn the Fox Who Was Afraid of the Dark, introduces Finn, Pip, Harry and Olive and begins The Bramblewood Series.

“May you always discover that even the darkest woods can be full of wonder.”
